## Mechanism gate and follow-up

The original one-report A32 equal-history gate failed: 117 baseline regions versus
118 candidate regions, so timing was not started. A64 had 111 identically ordered
regions in both reports. Two A32 diagnostic repeats then produced 118 baseline
and 116 candidate regions. Their full descriptor unions matched across
implementations; both variable entries were fallback regions. This separately
registered mechanism follow-up passed, without changing the original failure.

The strengthened offline audit requires unique entry descriptors, unchanged
region shapes/masks, no per-region growth, and restricts every size change to a
structured region with an omitted block. Its result:

```text
cache-pruning mechanism PASS a32Pairs=4 a32Varying=2 a64Shrinking=52 originalGate=FAILED
```

A64 generated bytes fell from 708,917 to 675,969 (-32,948), across 52 shrinking
structured regions with omitted blocks. This is a static mechanism observation,
not a throughput claim. A32 whole-run byte totals are not compared in this gate
because their region populations vary.

Raw reports are compressed individually under `evidence/mechanism/`. The original
one-shot equal-history comparison output was not separately retained. Its failure
is preserved by the preregistered plan and the audit's explicit `originalGate`
field. `comparison-initial-audit.json.gz` and `comparison-audited.json.gz` are the
initial and strengthened repeat/union audit outputs; they are deeply identical
because the strengthened checks did not change any real-report verdict. Neither
